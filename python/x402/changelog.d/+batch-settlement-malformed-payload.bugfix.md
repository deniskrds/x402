The batch-settlement EVM facilitator now rejects malformed deposit, voucher, refund, claim and settle payloads (missing or wrongly typed fields, invalid addresses, salts or hex signatures, numeric fields that are not uint256 decimal strings, a non-integer `withdrawDelay`) with the payload type's reason, such as `invalid_batch_settlement_evm_deposit_payload`, instead of raising. `invalid_message` / `error_message` names the malformed field.
